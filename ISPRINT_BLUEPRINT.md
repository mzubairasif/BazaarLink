# ibex. iSprint Challenge — Round 2: The Blueprint
**Theme:** Make Karachi Great Again · Karachi 2026
**Stakes:** PKR 1,750,000 Total Prize Pool (Up to PKR 1,000,000 for Winner)

---

## 🏆 Round 2 Mission Statement
> *"You identified the problem. Now build the solution."*
> Build a detailed, executable solution that works in the real Karachi context, runs without external dependency, and can be replicated beyond the first community it serves. Show us the execution, the resources, the sustainability, and the scale.

---

## 📊 The 6 Evaluation Criteria

| # | Criteria | What the Jury Looks For | BazaarLink Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **01** | **Solution Clarity** | Is the solution clearly defined and easy to understand without jargon? | Simple 3-step loop: Buyer broadcasts query → Vendors submit live bids → Buyer accepts & receives E-Receipt with matching claim code. |
| **02** | **Execution Feasibility** | Can this actually be done? Is the plan realistic given Karachi's context? | Fully working Kotlin Jetpack Compose Android app backed by real-time Firebase Firestore, Firebase Storage, and offline caching. |
| **03** | **Self-Sustainability** | Does it sustain itself without external funding or government support? | Monetized via Vendor Connects economy (vendors pay micro-credits for submitting bids) + premium shop verification badges. |
| **04** | **Scalability Logic** | Does the model replicate cleanly across neighborhoods without rebuilding? | Hyperlocal zonal structure: Starts in Saddar (Star City, Regal, Cooperative Market) → Replicates to Tariq Road, Urdu Bazar, Hyderi, and Clifton. |
| **05** | **Problem-Solution Fit** | Does it address the real Karachi root cause identified in Round 1? | Eliminates physical market fatigue, price gouging, and unorganized inventory in dense Karachi wholesale markets. |
| **06** | **Awareness of Gaps** | Are blind spots and risks acknowledged and mitigated? | Addressed merchant smartphone literacy (voice notes support), fake quotes, and basement internet connectivity (offline caching). |

## 🚫 Common Mistakes to Avoid (ibex Directives)

- ❌ **Do NOT change your problem:** Round 2 builds directly on Round 1.
- ❌ **Do NOT make it overly ambitious:** Simple & executable beats complex & vague.
- ❌ **Do NOT depend on external funding:** Must be self-sustaining from day one.
- ❌ **Do NOT skip the Risk Analysis:** Name blind spots and mitigations explicitly.
- ❌ **Do NOT exceed 10 slides:** Strict 10-slide limit for presentation.
- ❌ **Do NOT be vague about resources:** Clear cost, tech stack, and timeline breakdown.

---

## 📁 Slide Presentation Structure (10 Slides Max)

- **Slide 01:** Cover (Team Name, University, BazaarLink, Round 2 Blueprint)
- **Slide 02:** Problem Recap (Karachi's unorganized markets & buyer friction in 1 paragraph)
- **Slide 03:** The Solution (BazaarLink reverse-marketplace explained simply)
- **Slide 04:** How It Works (Step-by-step buyer broadcast → vendor bid → e-receipt deal loop)
- **Slide 05:** Who Is Involved (Buyers, Saddar Merchants, Market Associations)
- **Slide 06:** Resources Needed (People, time, cloud infrastructure costs, rollout plan)
- **Slide 07:** Sustainability (Vendor Connects model & self-sustaining revenue stream)
- **Slide 08:** Scalability (Saddar → Tariq Road → Urdu Bazar → All Karachi Markets)
- **Slide 09:** Risks & Challenges (Vendor tech adoption, trust verification, fake bids & solutions)
- **Slide 10:** The Ask (What is needed to take BazaarLink live across Karachi)
